# Code of Conduct

This project is for collaborative technical work. Keep discussion respectful, specific, and focused on improving the software.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct without being hostile.
- Give technical feedback with enough context to act on.
- Assume mistakes are fixable and clarify intent when something is ambiguous.
- Respect boundaries around identity, experience level, location, and availability.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or sustained personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Discriminatory remarks or exclusionary behavior.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing issues, reviews, or discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, block accounts, or take other reasonable action when behavior makes the project unsafe or unproductive.

If you need to report a concern, contact the maintainer through the repository owner profile or another private channel available on GitHub. Include relevant links, screenshots, or context where possible.
